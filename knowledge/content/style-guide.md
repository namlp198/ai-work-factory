# Content style guide

Edit this file to match your brand; every content agent reads it.

## Voice
- Clear, concrete and friendly. Explain, do not sell. Short sentences, active voice.
- Vietnamese content: natural Vietnamese, full diacritics, no machine-translated phrasing. Keep common
  technical terms in English where Vietnamese readers use them (API, server, deploy), explain them once.
- Address the reader directly ("bạn"); avoid "chúng tôi" unless the piece speaks for the company.

## Accuracy
- Every number, date, price, quote and customer name must come from a source. No source, no claim.
- No superlatives that cannot be proven ("tốt nhất", "số 1", "the best").
- Never compare with a named competitor.

## Format
- One idea per paragraph; headings every 150-300 words in long pieces.
- Lists for steps and options; tables only when comparing.
- End with one clear call to action.
